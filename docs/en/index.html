No gaming PC?
Play VRChat in PC VR anyway.

Geaspec is a community platform in Japan. People with a standalone VR headset book time on someone's idle gaming PC and play VRChat in PC VR: the PC renders VRChat and streams it to their own headset, and they log in with their own account. We are currently running a small, free, invite-only test. To join, apply through our Discord server.

Join the test on Discord

How to join the test

  1. 01Join our DiscordJoin the Geaspec Discord server.
  2. 02Apply as a testerFollow the guide in the server to apply as a tester.
  3. 03Book and playThe bot sends you your own link to the booking site. Book a time, then follow the step-by-step checklist on your booking page.

The test is currently run in Japanese, for players in Japan.

How it works

  1. 01Book a PCPick an available time slot on a PC that someone has made available.
  2. 02Connect from your headsetConnect to that PC remotely with Virtual Desktop and log in to VRChat with your own account.
  3. 03Hand it backWhen time is up, the PC returns to its owner and your login data and photos are deleted automatically.

The VR video goes directly between the PC and your headset. Geaspec's servers only handle booking and guidance.

Why we are building this

  • Many PC-only VRChat worlds and avatars cannot be fully seen on a standalone headset, but a VRChat-recommended PC costs ¥429,980 or more in Japan (Dospara listings, October 2026). For many students and young players, that is out of reach.
  • Meanwhile, most gaming PCs are used only a few hours a day.
  • Japan is one of VRChat's largest communities: it ranked first in visits to the official VRChat website in 2025 (Sensor Tower, via MoguraVR), and avatar and 3D model sales on BOOTH reached ¥10.4 billion in 2025 (pixiv). Much of this content is made for PC.

Supported headsets

Any standalone VR headset that can use Virtual Desktop's "Allow Remote Connections" option.

  • Meta Quest series
  • Pico series
  • HTC Vive XR Elite, and others

Keeping accounts safe

  • Users always log in to VRChat with their own VRChat account.
  • Geaspec never asks for, stores, or shares anyone's password.
  • When a session ends, login data and photos are deleted from the PC automatically.
  • During a session, the lender's screen is covered, so the lender cannot see what the player is doing.
  • The test is invite- and referral-only, so only people known to the community take part.

Current status

Geaspec is in a small, free, invite-only test. We do not charge fees, use VRChat logos or official images, or advertise inside VRChat. We are in contact with the relevant platforms, including VRChat and Steam, and will only offer a paid service in a way their terms allow.
