No gaming PC?
Play PC VRChat anyway.

Geaspec lets people with a standalone VR headset play the PC version of VRChat by borrowing a gaming PC from another person for a booked time slot. We are currently running a free test. To join the test, apply through our Discord server.

Join the test on Discord

How to join the test

  1. 01Join our DiscordJoin the Geaspec Discord server.
  2. 02Apply as a testerFollow the guide in the server to apply as a tester.
  3. 03Open the booking siteFollow the bot's steps, and you will receive your own link to the booking site.

Anyone can open the booking site, but the test steps and support are given on Discord. If you apply through Discord, the steps for your test arrive in your thread after you book. The test is currently run in Japanese.

How it works

  1. 01Book a PCPick an available time slot on a PC that someone has made available.
  2. 02Connect from your headsetConnect to that PC remotely with Virtual Desktop.
  3. 03Hand it backWhen time is up, the PC returns to its owner and your login data and photos are deleted automatically.

Supported headsets

Any standalone VR headset that can use Virtual Desktop's "Allow Remote Connections" option.

  • Meta Quest series
  • Pico series
  • HTC Vive XR Elite, and others

Keeping accounts safe

  • Users always log in to VRChat with their own VRChat account.
  • Geaspec never asks for, stores, or shares anyone's password.
  • When a session ends, login data and photos are deleted from the PC automatically.

Current status

Geaspec is in a free test. We plan to offer it as a paid service in the future, and we are contacting the relevant platforms, including Steam and VRChat, to make sure we follow their terms.
