How it works

  1. Book

    Pick an open time slot on a PC.{% if free_mode %} Free during the test.{% endif %}

  2. Connect and play

    Connect with Virtual Desktop and log in to VRChat with your own account.

  3. Ends automatically

    When time is up, the session ends and your login data is deleted.

The VR video goes directly between the PC and your headset. Geaspec's servers only handle booking.

About Geaspec

Geaspec is an independent project from Japan. We are running a small, free, invite-only test with players in Japan, and the booking site is currently in Japanese.

We do not charge fees, use VRChat logos or official images, or advertise inside VRChat. We are in contact with VRChat and Steam, and will only offer a paid service in a way their terms allow. Geaspec is not affiliated with VRChat, Valve, Meta or Virtual Desktop.
